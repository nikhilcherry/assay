# Innovation Claim: Evidence That Knows Its Own Weight

## 1. The Core Idea
Under Policy v1.0, wrongly blocking or reporting a legitimate customer (`BLOCK_CARD`, `BLOCK_ALL_CARDS`, `FILE_REPORT`) breaches R1 and scores zero. Assay introduces **Evidence That Knows Its Own Weight** (`assay/r2/investigate_r2.py`, `assay/r2/finding.py`):
1. Every candidate detector and segmenter is backtested strictly as-of (`t <= opened_at`) across 4,675 confirmed-fraud episodes and 5,000–6,000 seeded July–October 2016 legitimate card-days.
2. Only findings with a measured likelihood ratio (`LR`) and bounded false-positive rate join `ENABLED` in the log-odds ledger with a re-runnable `Attestation` (`query`, `params`, exact IDs, dataset hash).
3. **Published Measured Negatives**: When a backtest shows a heuristic or model loses to baseline or harms legitimate customers (`LR <= 1.0`), Assay refuses to enable it and publishes the negative result.

## 2. Where Measured Negatives Prevent Errors
- **Per-Card Micro-Charges Rejected (`docs/r2/B6_micro_fanin.md`)**: Single-card slow drips (>= 4 sub-`$6.50` `ProductCD == 'C'` charges over `21.2` days) yield `LR = 0.63` (`1.62%` of legitimate card-days vs. `1.03%` of fraud) and are refused. Redesigning `MicroCharge` as a 30-day cohort burst (`14` cards on `HHF-004`, `HHF-005`) cuts legitimate card-day false positives to `0.0000%` (0 / 5,000).
- **Browser Bursts & Out-of-Episode Links Filtered (`docs/r2/B6_micro_fanin.md`)**: `device_profile` bursts fired on `HHF-014` and `HHF-016` from browser updates and were dropped. Restricting `FanIn` to non-freemail `R_emaildomain` within 72h catches `HHF-006` (`C01727-K2`, txn `3660263`, `$312.68` to `sc.rr.com`, 17 first-time cards in 21d vs. `0.75` baseline mean, `lr=3.0` disclosed as `ASSUMPTION`), while demoting `HHF-013` (`$409.30` `W` purchase outside 72h) to `kind='context'`, `lr=1.0`.
- **Issuer-Bucket Innocence Signals Gate-Kept (`docs/r2/B29_innocence.md`)**: Because `card_id`s are high-volume issuer buckets, `home_region` (`23.0%` fraud vs. `7.8%` cleared), `stable_profile` (`53.4%` vs. `23.7%`), and `long_tenure_clean` (`LR_alert = 0.7420`, 95% CI `[0.5190, 1.0607]`) fail the `< 0.50` keep gate; uncalibrated LRs are withheld while Policy R7 recurring matches (`7.3%` cleared) remain available.
- **HMM Segmenter Refused vs. Dispute-Episode Builder Enabled (`docs/r2/B34_episode.md`, `docs/r2/N7_dispute_episode.md`)**: The two-state HMM episode segmenter lost to the flagged-only baseline on closed cases and is not enabled; by contrast, the dispute-episode builder raises exact-match accuracy to `0.8061` vs. `0.7135` for the Round 1 window rule and is enabled.

## 3. Where Calibrated Evidence & Guards Change Outcomes
- **Capped Graph Rings (`docs/r2/B15_rings.md`)**: Dropping elements shared by > 15 cards in 30d and capping rings at 30 cards stops issuer buckets from merging. `ring_wcc` fires on `2.63%` of fraud vs. `0.30%` of legitimate card-days (`LR` `8.6946x`, 95% CI `[5.0934x, 14.8419x]`), catching a 12-card ring (`$29,568.99` volume, 7 prior fraud cases) on `HHF-005` (`C00348-K1`).
- **Per-Signature Campaign Calibration (`docs/r2/B28_campaign.md`)**: Calibrating 21d charge-shape cohorts on 6,000 legitimate card-days holds false positives to `0.0333%` (2 / 6,000), acts against 0 / 910 (`0.0000%`) cleared cases, and yields `LR` `45.36`—catching `HHF-001` (12 cards), `HHF-002` (14), `HHF-003` (8), `HHF-004` (13), and `HHF-005` (13) while rejecting `LR = 1.54` signatures on `HHF-006`, `HHF-009`, `HHF-016`.
- **Fused Graph + Vector Precedent (`docs/r2/B14_vector.md`)**: On 1,547 held-out Nov–Dec cases (`83.84%` fraud baseline), 2-hop traversal fused with 24-dim `vectorSearch` (`similar_cases_fused`) reaches `84.68%` accuracy and `0.1190` Brier (vs. `0.1656` for `risk_score`), flipping 434 baseline verdicts (`74.88%` right) and cutting cleared cases acted against from 173 to 165.
- **January Model Drift Guard (`docs/r2/B1_model.md`, `assay/r2/investigate_r2.py`)**: Only Jul–Oct 2016 are fully labelled (`2.8865%`–`4.1882%` named; `3.2693%` Oct fraud rate vs. `0.0248%` Nov, `0.1141%` Dec). While `model_r2` reaches `0.9206` Oct OOT ROC-AUC (`0.4936` PR-AUC, `0.0215` Brier, `0.00069` ECE vs. `risk_score` `0.8660` AUC, `0.0544` Brier), 4 top-25 features drift above `PSI > 0.25` in Jan (`D1n` `2.2558`, `uid_n` `1.7469`, `uid_amt_std` `0.7825`, `card_id_n` `0.7376`), leaving `1.0670%` of Jan rows `in_distribution`. `model_trusted` blocks low Jan scores (`0.0400` on `HHF-004`, `HHF-005`) from acting as innocence evidence while keeping isolated alerts (`HHF-017` `$43.92`, `HHF-018` `$34.22`) at R1 verification.
